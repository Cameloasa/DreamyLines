My Little Universe

A poem for DreamyLines

I keep the sunrise in my hands,
The ocean's whispers on the sand.
A book, a laugh, a sky of blue,
A thousand little dreams come true.

Soft pink for words I cannot say,
Gold for love that lights my day.
Deep blue for thoughts that wander far,
And silver hopes beneath each star.

For life is more than grand design,
It's little moments, yours and mine.
And if you ask what makes me whole,
It's simple things that touch the soul.
